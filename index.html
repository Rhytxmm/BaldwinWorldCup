Tournament setup

Fast games, full squad energy.

01

5v5 on the field

Five players play at a time, so every touch matters and the pace stays high.

02

One sub included

Each team gets one substitute, making the full roster six players total.

03

Knockout bracket

Win and advance. Lose and your run is done. Team names can be added when the field is set.

04

8-minute games

Each game is two 4-minute halves with a 2-minute halftime between them.

05

Refs and recorders

Every match will have 2 refs and 2 recorders keeping the game organized.
